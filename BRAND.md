# IFPÊ Bloom — Brand Reference

## Company
Tech & data consultancy based in London, UK, helping small and medium
businesses grow through personalised, strategic, human-centred
technology solutions — from concept to delivery.

Brand attributes: professional, accessible, wise, trustworthy.
Site language: English (UK).

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Yellow (primary) | #F4C21E | Accent, CTAs, highlights |
| Green (support) | #6A7F4B | Secondary accent, icons |
| Light beige (support) | #F3EEE3 | Alternate section background |
| Olive/brown (support) | #767363 | Secondary text |
| Light grey (neutral) | #D9D9D9 | Borders, dividers |
| Dark grey (neutral) | #494843 | Primary text, dark sections |

## Typography
- Display / headings: **Rethink Sans** (Google Fonts, free for commercial use)
- Body / long text: **Source Serif 4** (Google Fonts, free for commercial use)

## Logo
- Symbol: abstracted ipê-amarelo (yellow trumpet tree) flower — growth,
  resilience, blooming. Curves subtly reference Brasília Cathedral and sunset.
- Two lockups: circular emblem (badge) and horizontal wordmark.
- Never distort, recolor, or change the logo's proportions/spacing.
- Always keep clear space around the logo (protection area = symbol's
  circular diameter).
- Use color version on white/brand backgrounds; use white version on
  dark or low-contrast backgrounds.

## Voice
Professional but approachable. Balances technical credibility with
clear, human language. Avoid jargon-heavy or overly corporate tone.
